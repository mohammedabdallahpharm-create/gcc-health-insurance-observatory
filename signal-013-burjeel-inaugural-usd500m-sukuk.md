# Signal 013 — Burjeel Completes USD 500 Million Inaugural Sukuk

**Record ID:** UAE-HI-013  
**Market:** UAE / GCC issuer; international debt-market transaction  
**Primary radar:** Market & Capital  
**Connected radar:** Provider  
**Issue date:** 1 July 2026  
**Announcement date:** 2 July 2026  
**Evidence status:** Completed issuance disclosed by the issuer through ADX.  
**Confidence:** High for issuance terms; use of proceeds should be interpreted carefully.

## Observation

Burjeel Holdings completed its inaugural USD 500 million, five-year senior unsecured Sukuk issuance under its USD 1.5 billion Trust Certificate Issuance Programme. The Sukuk carries a 7.000% profit rate and was priced at a 7.125% yield.

The issuer stated that net proceeds would be used in line with the offering documents, including refinancing existing bank facilities and supporting strategic priorities. The transaction therefore represents a completed capital-markets financing event, but it should not be described as USD 500 million of new expansion capital.

## Primary evidence

- [Burjeel Holdings — issuer announcement hosted by Abu Dhabi Securities Exchange, 2 July 2026](https://apigateway.adx.ae/adx/cdn/1.0/content/download/5086475)

## What the evidence supports

- USD 500 million of senior unsecured Sukuk was issued on 1 July 2026.
- The instrument has a five-year tenor and is due in 2031.
- The profit rate is 7.000%; the issue yield is 7.125%.
- The issuance sits under a USD 1.5 billion programme.
- The issuer's stated uses include refinancing and strategic priorities.

## What it does not establish

- That the full proceeds are available for new projects or capacity expansion.
- The exact allocation between refinancing and other strategic uses from the announcement alone.
- Any causal link between the Sukuk and a specific hospital project or operating outcome.

## Strategic interpretation — hypothesis

The transaction demonstrates access to international debt capital by a UAE healthcare operator. It is relevant to the observatory because financing structure and cost of capital can influence the pace and form of provider expansion, refinancing and strategic investment.

## Monitoring tests

1. Reconcile proceeds and repayment allocation against interim and annual financial statements.
2. Track leverage, maturity profile, finance costs and capital expenditure.
3. Identify any named projects explicitly funded by the proceeds before making project-level links.

## Classification

**Market & Capital:** debt capital / Sukuk issuance / refinancing and strategic financing.  
**Provider:** healthcare-group financing capacity.
