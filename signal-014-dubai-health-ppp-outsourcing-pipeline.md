# Signal 014 — Dubai Health PPP Pipeline Includes Laundry and CSSD Outsourcing Projects

**Record ID:** UAE-HI-014  
**Market:** Dubai, United Arab Emirates  
**Primary radar:** Market & Capital  
**Connected radar:** Provider  
**Observation date:** 12 November 2026  
**Evidence status:** Official PPP pipeline lists two healthcare support-service projects in Procurement. Values are indicative; no award or signed contract is established by the pipeline page.  
**Confidence:** High for the published project descriptions, indicative values and displayed phase; low for award timing and final commercial terms.

## Observation

Dubai's Department of Finance PPP Projects Pipeline lists two Dubai Health projects involving private-sector delivery of hospital support services:

1. **Healthcare facility laundry services:** indicative project value AED 200 million; PPP model BOT; current phase shown as Procurement. The project description refers to building and operating laundry services for Dubai Health facilities, with potential future extension to private healthcare entities.
2. **Central Sterile Supply Department (CSSD):** indicative project value AED 80 million; PPP model BOT; current phase shown as Procurement. The project description refers to a third party building and operating sterilisation services for Dubai Health facilities, with potential future extension to private healthcare entities.

The combined indicative value is AED 280 million. This is a pipeline value, not an awarded contract value or confirmed private investment.

## Primary evidence

- [Dubai Department of Finance — PPP Projects Pipeline](https://www.dof.gov.ae/en-us/ppp/Pages/PPP_Projects.aspx)

## What the evidence supports

- Both projects are listed by the Dubai Department of Finance.
- Dubai Health is the implementing entity.
- The displayed current phase for both is Procurement.
- The indicative values are AED 200 million for laundry and AED 80 million for CSSD.
- The stated PPP model is Build–Operate–Transfer (BOT).

## What it does not establish

- That tenders have been awarded, contracts signed or construction started.
- That the indicative values represent final CAPEX, total contract value or committed public/private expenditure.
- That the projects are already serving private healthcare facilities.
- That the projects have generated savings, service improvements or workforce changes.

## Strategic interpretation — hypothesis

These projects indicate that healthcare support functions may be packaged as long-term PPP operating opportunities rather than delivered solely through each hospital's internal infrastructure. The potential extension to private healthcare entities makes the operating model worth monitoring, but it is not yet evidence of a market-wide outsourcing shift.

## Monitoring tests

1. Capture tender notices, prequalification documents, RFPs, bid deadlines and award announcements.
2. Identify selected consortium/operator, contract tenor, financing structure and risk allocation.
3. Track construction, commissioning and service commencement.
4. Verify whether extension to private providers is included in the awarded scope or remains an option.

## Classification

**Market & Capital:** PPP pipeline / procurement / healthcare outsourcing.  
**Provider:** shared hospital support infrastructure and operations.
