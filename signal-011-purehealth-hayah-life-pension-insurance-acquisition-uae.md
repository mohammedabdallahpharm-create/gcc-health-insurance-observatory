## 1. External Signal Detection

On 24 November 2026, PureHealth announced that it had entered into an agreement to acquire a **67.5% controlling stake** in Hayah Insurance Company PJSC through its insurance subsidiary, The National Insurance Company – Daman PJSC.

PureHealth describes the transaction as a step toward building a diversified, multi-line insurance platform. If completed, the acquisition would extend the Group's insurance capabilities beyond health and property & casualty into life insurance, pension management and long-term savings.

The announcement positions the move within PureHealth's broader Care and Cover model: healthcare and health insurance are to be complemented by products intended to help people protect income during working years and build savings for retirement.

This is a distinct corporate action from the ownership and claims-decision questions documented in Signal UAE-HI-009. Signal 009 remains the canonical record for PureHealth's existing vertical integration and claims-decision-control investigation; this record documents the separate Hayah transaction and its potential effect on the Group's insurance portfolio.

## 2. Signal Documentation & Evidence

### A. Transaction terms announced

PureHealth's official announcement states that:

- PureHealth entered into an agreement to acquire a 67.5% controlling stake in Hayah.
- The acquisition is to be made through Daman.
- Hayah is a UAE-based digital-first insurer, formerly known as AXA Green Crescent Insurance Company PJSC.
- Hayah serves more than 200,000 covered lives and more than 1,000 corporate clients, according to the announcement.
- Hayah's stated portfolio includes life insurance, pension management, loan protection, retirement and long-term savings solutions for individuals and corporates.

**Transaction status:** This is an announced agreement, not evidence that the acquisition has completed. The transaction remains subject to customary closing conditions, including relevant regulatory approvals. PureHealth says further details will be announced upon completion.

### B. Proposed business and operating scope

PureHealth states that, upon completion:

- Hayah will become part of the Group's broader insurance platform.
- Daman will deliver the expanded offering, including retirement savings plans for individuals and workplace savings programmes for employers.
- Hayah will retain its digital operating model, management team and customer relationships.

These are stated transaction intentions. The announcement does not yet establish the final operating model, product-launch timetable, distribution arrangements, integration milestones or the allocation of responsibilities between Daman and Hayah after closing.

### C. Strategic rationale stated by PureHealth

The Group frames the acquisition as an expansion from healthcare and health insurance toward broader financial wellbeing across a person's life course. It specifically refers to income protection during working years, end-of-service benefit arrangements, structured pension management and long-term retirement savings.

PureHealth also cites a UAE life-insurance market estimate of approximately USD 2.5 billion and a global market estimate of USD 3 trillion. These are figures presented in the company's announcement; this record does not independently validate the market-sizing methodology.

## 4. Impact & Opportunity Monitoring

### Competing hypotheses

**Hypothesis 1 — A broader employee and individual protection relationship**

If Daman can distribute Hayah's life, workplace savings and pension capabilities through employer relationships, the Group may be able to serve a wider set of employee financial-protection needs than health coverage alone.

**Test:** named employer programmes, product availability, employer participation, covered employees, distribution agreements and renewal behaviour.

**Hypothesis 2 — Multi-line insurance diversification**

The acquisition may accelerate Daman's evolution from a health-led insurer toward a broader multi-line insurance platform spanning health, property & casualty, life and related savings/protection solutions.

**Test:** post-completion legal and operating structure, licensed product lines, published product portfolio, premium mix and segment disclosures.

**Hypothesis 3 — Longer-duration customer relationships**

Life, pension and long-term savings products may create customer and employer relationships extending beyond annual health-policy cycles.

**Test:** product duration, persistency, contribution continuity, customer retention and disclosed recurring revenue or assets under administration.

**Hypothesis 4 — Strategic ambition without immediate operational integration**

The acquisition may initially add product capability and market access while the businesses continue to operate through distinct systems, teams, licences and distribution channels.

**Test:** integration announcements, governance arrangements, systems changes, management appointments and explicit cross-selling or servicing processes.

These are hypotheses to test, not findings of realised synergies or a completed integrated platform.

### What is established

- PureHealth announced an agreement to acquire a 67.5% controlling stake in Hayah through Daman.
- Hayah's stated product portfolio includes life insurance, pension management, loan protection, retirement and long-term savings.
- PureHealth says Hayah serves more than 200,000 covered lives and over 1,000 corporate clients.
- PureHealth says Daman will deliver the expanded offering, including individual retirement savings plans and workplace savings programmes.
- The transaction is subject to customary closing conditions, including regulatory approvals.

### What remains unverified

1. Regulatory approval and legal completion of the acquisition.
2. Final ownership structure and any retained minority interests after closing.
3. The effective date on which Hayah becomes part of the operating group.
4. The detailed division of product, underwriting, administration, distribution and customer-servicing responsibilities between Daman and Hayah.
5. Which new or existing products will be distributed through Daman's employer and individual channels.
6. Whether health insurance, life insurance and savings products will be bundled or sold through coordinated journeys.
7. Employer participation, employee enrolment and individual take-up after completion.
8. Any changes to systems, data governance, claims/benefit administration or customer consent arrangements.
9. Financial contribution, premium mix, assets under management/administration, profitability and realised synergies.
10. Any measurable change in customer retention, persistency or lifetime relationship value.

Absence of public evidence at announcement stage is not evidence that these arrangements will not occur. It means they cannot yet be verified.

### Follow-up monitoring tests

- Track regulatory approvals, exchange disclosures and the formal completion announcement.
- Review Hayah and Daman product pages, policy wording and licensing disclosures after closing.
- Monitor Daman announcements for workplace savings, end-of-service and retirement product launches.
- Look for named employer partnerships, procurement activity and benefit-programme adoption.
- Track leadership, legal-entity, governance and operating-model changes.
- Examine future financial statements for premium mix, segment reporting, assets under management/administration and acquisition-related disclosures.
- Monitor whether customer and employer distribution is coordinated across health, life and savings products; do not infer cross-selling from common ownership alone.

## 5. Regulatory and Jurisdictional Boundary

The transaction concerns UAE-licensed insurance entities and is expressly stated to be subject to relevant regulatory approvals. The announcement does not identify every required approval, confirm that any approval has been granted, or establish the transaction's completion date.

Pension management, workplace savings and end-of-service arrangements should not be treated as interchangeable with health insurance. Their applicable regulatory permissions, product structures, custody/investment arrangements, employer obligations and customer protections require separate examination.

This record does not infer that the acquisition changes UAE mandatory health-insurance rules or automatically combines health, life and pension products into one policy.

## 6. Central Observatory Question

**Can PureHealth and Daman translate the acquisition of Hayah into a genuinely coordinated health, life and long-term financial-protection proposition for individuals and employers — or will the businesses remain a diversified portfolio of distinct products and operating models?**

## 7. Working Conclusion

The 24 November 2026 announcement is a verified corporate-expansion signal: PureHealth has agreed, through Daman, to acquire a 67.5% controlling stake in Hayah. The stated scope extends the Group's insurance portfolio into life insurance, pension management, loan protection, retirement and long-term savings.

The announcement supports a strategic direction toward connecting health coverage with broader financial protection across working life and retirement. It does **not** yet prove that an integrated lifetime proposition exists, that products are bundled, that employers have adopted workplace savings programmes, or that financial synergies have been realised.

**Working thesis:** Competitive positioning among large UAE healthcare and insurance groups may increasingly involve the ability to connect healthcare access, health coverage, income protection and long-term savings. Whether this is achieved through ownership, coordinated distribution or integrated customer journeys remains an open question.
