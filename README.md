# GCC Health Insurance Observatory

A regional, evidence-led observatory interface for tracking signals, structural changes and market evolution across the six GCC health-insurance markets.

## Navigation model

The GCC landing page is the regional entry point. The GCC signal map connects to six country observatories. Every country uses the same shared observation framework and navigation pattern; selecting a country changes the market label only.

## Shared observation framework

1. Regulatory & Policy
2. Insurance Companies
3. TPAs & Claims Operations
4. Healthcare Providers
5. Market & Commercial
6. Digital Health & AI
7. People & Leadership

The framework is consistent across all six markets. Country-specific entities, signals and sources are added only after research and evidence review. A shared framework does not imply that market research is complete or that signals have been detected.

## Evidence and limitations

Map markers, sample signal cards and registry rows are illustrative interface content only. They are not verified market events or sourced research. The regional map is schematic and not geospatial.

No live monitoring, automated ingestion, database, API or evidence-verification workflow is implemented in this interface-only release.

## Research principles

- Observe, detect, document, connect and track.
- Preserve original sources and distinguish event date from observation date.
- Separate reported information from verified evidence.
- Do not forecast market outcomes or infer causality from coincidence.
- Human review remains responsible for evidence verification and interpretation.

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a browser. No build step, backend, API or database is required.

## Roadmap

Future releases may add country-level evidence registries, structured source records, entity relationships, historical observations, GCC cross-market comparison and AI-assisted signal discovery. These capabilities are not yet implemented.
