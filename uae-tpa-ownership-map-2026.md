## 2. Ownership relationships supported by published evidence

### A. Daman Healthcare Solutions — insurer-owned TPA

- The CBUAE register identifies Daman Healthcare Solutions - L.L.C - O.P.C as a licensed TPA.
- PureHealth's interim consolidated financial statements for the period ended 30 September 2026 list Daman Healthcare Solutions - LLC under The National Insurance Company (Daman) PJSC, with 100% ownership.
- The same statements list Daman as a subsidiary of PureHealth. The group relationship is therefore documented as PureHealth → Daman → Daman Healthcare Solutions (TPA).
- This establishes ownership in the reporting structure; it does not disclose a separate purchase price or new capital injection into the TPA during 2026.

Sources:
- [PureHealth interim financial statements, 30 September 2026 — ADX PDF](https://apigateway.adx.ae/adx/cdn/1.0/content/download/5848889) (notes 2.2, pages 17–18)
- [CBUAE Register — September 2026](https://centralbank.ae/media/os1jf13r/cb-register-september-of-2026.pdf)

### B. MedNet Global Healthcare Solutions — Munich Re group

- The CBUAE register lists MedNet Global Healthcare Solutions LLC as a UAE-licensed TPA.
- Munich Re's 2025 Company Annual Report lists MedNet Global Healthcare Solutions LLC, Abu Dhabi, with 100% of share capital held.
- This is an evidenced foreign-insurer/reinsurer-group ownership relationship. The report is a 2025 year-end ownership baseline, not evidence of a 2026 acquisition or change.

Source:
- [Munich Re Annual Report — Company 2025 (PDF)](https://www.munichre.com/content/dam/munichre/mrwebsiteslaunches/2025-annual-report/MunichRe-Annual-Report-Company-2025-en.pdf/_jcr_content/renditions/original./MunichRe-Annual-Report-Company-2025-en.pdf)
- [CBUAE Register — September 2026](https://centralbank.ae/media/os1jf13r/cb-register-september-of-2026.pdf)

### C. Nextcare Claims Management — Allianz Partners group

- The CBUAE register lists Nextcare Claims Management LLC as a UAE-licensed TPA.
- Nextcare's official privacy notice describes Nextcare Claims Management LLC as an indirect subsidiary of Allianz Partners SAS.
- The notice establishes the stated group relationship, but does not disclose the UAE operating company's exact direct shareholder chain or percentage in the notice reviewed. Treat this as parent-group evidence, not a complete legal shareholding chart.

Source:
- [Nextcare official privacy notice](https://www.nextcarehealth.com/privacy-notice/)
- [CBUAE Register — September 2026](https://centralbank.ae/media/os1jf13r/cb-register-september-of-2026.pdf)

### D. Aspire Solutions Operations — RGA-linked minority ownership

- The CBUAE register lists Aspire Solutions Operations Health Insurance Claims Management LLC as a UAE-licensed TPA.
- RGA's 2024 Financial Condition Report organizational chart states that Aspire Health Management Solutions Holding RSC Ltd owns 49% of Aspire Solutions Operations Health Insurance Claims Management LLC; an external party owns 51%.
- The report places the holding company within RGA Partnerships LLC's group structure. The identity of the external 51% holder is not disclosed in the chart.
- This is a documented minority ownership structure, not evidence of a 2026 transaction.

Source:
- [RGA Global Re Financial Condition Report 2024 — Appendix A, organizational chart (PDF)](https://www.rgare.com/docs/default-source/regulatory-documents/rga-global-re-year-ended-31-december-2024-financial-condition-report.pdf)
- [CBUAE Register — September 2026](https://centralbank.ae/media/os1jf13r/cb-register-september-of-2026.pdf)

## 3. Historical private-equity transaction — NAS / Neuron

- Arcapita and Bahrain Mumtalakat acquired an equity stake in NAS United Healthcare Services in 2017. Arcapita's published history describes the stake as approximately 90%.
- During the holding period, NAS combined with Neuron LLC to form NAS Neuron.
- Arcapita and Mumtalakat announced the sale of their stake in NAS Neuron to an unnamed US-based Fortune 500 company in August 2020. Arcapita's subsequent firm-history page records the exit as completed in 2021, so the precise completion date should be treated cautiously pending transaction-closing documentation.
- This is historical private-equity investment and exit evidence. It does not identify the current ultimate owner of NAS Administration Services LLC or Neuron LLC.

Sources:
- [Arcapita — NAS investment case study](https://www.arcapita.com/what-we-do/success-stories/nas/)
- [Arcapita — Exit announcement, 17 August 2020](https://arcapita.com/insights/arcapita-exits-healthcare-investment/)
- [NAS — About us / company history](https://www.nas.ae/about-us/)

## 5. 2026 ownership-change detection

As of 13 November 2026, this research batch has not verified a completed 2026 change of control, acquisition, disposal, or private-equity investment in a UAE-licensed TPA.

This is a research finding with a defined evidence boundary, not proof that no such transaction exists.

Next verification steps:
1. Compare successive CBUAE register snapshots and identify new, removed, renamed, or reclassified legal entities.
2. Check ADX/DFM disclosures and parent-company interim/annual financial statements for changes in subsidiary lists, control, related-party transactions, and non-controlling interests.
3. Search official group filings and company legal notices for shareholder changes, capital increases, mergers, disposals, and beneficial-ownership updates.
4. Where public sources do not disclose ownership, keep the record marked "not publicly verified"; do not infer from management appointments, service partnerships, brand affiliation, or licensing alone.

## 6. Interpretation

The verified evidence illustrates at least three distinct ownership models in the UAE TPA landscape:
- Insurer-owned TPA: Daman Healthcare Solutions.
- Reinsurer/group-owned TPA: MedNet Global and the RGA-linked 49% Aspire holding.
- International assistance group subsidiary: Nextcare under Allianz Partners.

The NAS / Neuron case adds a historical private-equity investment-and-exit example.

These examples are not sufficient to conclude that the UAE TPA market is currently consolidating or experiencing a new investment cycle. A current trend claim requires verified 2026 transaction events and a more complete ownership census.

**Classification:** Structural ownership map / research baseline. Not a new 2026 transaction signal.
